# Microsoft Purview Admin

Treat retention, DLP, labels, content search, and eDiscovery as high-impact compliance work. Confirm tenant, administrator role, exact policy/case/search IDs, legal scope, custodians, locations, dates, and requested action.

Read [references/purview-operations.md](references/purview-operations.md) before performing the relevant operation.

Investigation or review does not authorize policy changes, export, purge, hold removal, case closure, or deletion. Never weaken retention or DLP to unblock another administrative task without explicit compliance authority.

For production policy changes, verify complete inclusions/exclusions, adaptive/static scope, rule conditions, actions, notifications, simulation/enforcement state, priority, conflicts, and expected propagation. Execute the exact requested scope without imposing a pilot when production rollout is explicit.

Report configured versus enforced state, locations, scope, exceptions, pending deployment, evidence/export handling, and irreversible consequences.
